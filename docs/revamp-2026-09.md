# Portfolio revamp (September 2026)

## Why

A review of the previous layout found that the content was not ordered by what a hiring manager needs:

- On a phone, Experience (30 bullets) and Testimonials (8 long quotes) took 64% of a 12,569px page. The Projects section took 9%.
- The strongest result, scrap cut from 5% to 0.5% ($15M a year), appeared only as a bold bullet and an unlabelled "0.5% scrap rate" stat.
- The hero named the job title but not the employer.
- The three GM roles were listed as unrelated jobs, so the promotions did not show.

## What changed

The charcoal, copper, DM Serif Display and DM Sans identity is kept. The structure is new.

- **Hero:** kicker naming the title and GM, a one-line positioning statement (from the existing "find the root cause…" line), an "Email me" button, a "Now" badge on the portrait, and a four-cell proof strip (8+, $15M, 0.5% down from 5%, 12 suppliers green).
- **Impact** (`#projects`): a featured case study with a to-scale before/after scrap chart; supplier turnaround with a 12-dot grid; EV launch and IATF certification cards. Every claim already appeared on the site.
- **Experience:** roles grouped under GM and Continental on a promotion line. Each role shows its key result, and the full responsibilities sit in a native `<details>` (works without JS; expanded for print).
- **Recommendations:** two featured quotes, one from each company, and six cards in a grid. On phones the six become a swipe row. Quote text is unchanged; one key phrase in each is highlighted.
- **About:** skills in three groups, plus a credentials row (lead auditor certification, MS, BS). `#education` still resolves.
- **Contact:** a large panel with the email address as the main link.
- **Other:** section separators replaced by numbered section heads, single-colour social icons, and the full-page grid limited to the hero and contact panel.

All old anchors still work: `#home #about #experience #projects #apps #education #testimonials #contact`.
