# Portfolio redesign (September 2026)

## Why

The first pass at this revamp kept the charcoal ground, copper accent and DM Serif Display. It also added a stat strip, numbered section labels, cards, gradient washes and a fade-in on every section. Those are the defaults of generated pages, not choices made for this subject, and the result looked templated.

This version starts from the subject instead. Vishut's work is taking a process that is out of control and bringing it back into control. So the page opens with that result, drawn the way a quality engineer would draw it.

## The design

- **One memorable thing: a staged scrap-rate chart.** The Spring Hill result is drawn as two stage levels on a real 0–6% axis: a red line at 5% before, a solid drop on the dashed stage break, and a green line at 0.5% after, with "$15 million saved a year" written in the space the drop opens up. It has no invented sample points, control limits or dates, and a note says the lines are stage levels and time is not to scale. It is built from HTML and CSS borders, not SVG, and draws itself once the first time it is seen. It is the only motion the visitor does not trigger.
- **Colour carries meaning, nothing else.** Red (`--oc`, out of control) appears only on the before line. Green (`--ic`, in control) appears only on the after line, the Email me button and the contact email. Links are ink with an underline.
- **Ground.** A cool, pale drafting-film grey (`#F2F4F3`) by default, and a complete night-shift dark theme (`#16212A`). Light is now the default. To go back to dark, change the fallback in `theme-init.js` and in `currentThemeMode()` in `script.js` to `'dark'`.
- **Type.** One family, Archivo, self-hosted as a single variable woff2. The width axis does the work: the name is set wide on desktop and narrows on phones instead of shrinking. Sentence case throughout; no all-caps labels.
- **Layout.** Left-aligned on a 12-column grid. No cards, shadows, gradients, eyebrows, numbering, middle-dot strings or arrows. Rules appear only under the nav when scrolled, between the two employers and above the footer.
- **Content.** Every claim traces to the previous site. All 30 experience bullets are kept verbatim (the key ones shown, the rest in native `<details>`). All eight recommendations are kept verbatim, grouped by company, and each employer links to its recommenders.

Section anchors are unchanged: `#home #projects #experience #testimonials #about #education #apps #contact`, plus `#voice-<surname>` for each recommendation.

## Checked

No horizontal overflow from 320 to 1440px. The name, role, credential, Email me and the whole chart fit in a 1440×900 first screen, and Email me fits in the first 390×844 phone screen. Also checked: light, dark and system themes, keyboard focus, reduced motion, no-JS rendering, print with all details expanded, nav highlighting, direct links and browser Back, and no console errors.

## Social card and icons

`og-image.png` (the LinkedIn/Slack preview), `icon.svg` and `apple-touch-icon.png` now match the page: Archivo on the drafting-film paper, the name, the role line and a small copy of the staged scrap chart. To rebuild the card after changing the hero, run `node assets/og/build.js`. It reads the h1 and `.hero-role` from index.html, and it refuses to build if the fonts are missing, if text would collide with the portrait, or if the page's chart no longer shows 5%, 0.5% and $15 million. The icon letters are Archivo outlines, so the icon does not depend on any installed font.

## Needs the owner's decision

- **Default theme.** Light is now the default, which reverses the dark default from b55f7c8. To go back, set `mode = 'dark'` in `theme-init.js`. In `script.js`, set `THEME_MODES = ['dark', 'light', 'system']`, change the `currentThemeMode()` fallback to `'dark'` and update the `THEME_UI` labels. Then set the toggle's initial label in index.html to "Theme: dark. Switch to light theme." and `<meta name="theme-color">` to `#16212A`.
- **Lead auditor dates.** The credential is dated 2021–2023 because it is listed under the Continental role (May 2021 – February 2023). The page words it as held in that role. Confirm whether it is still current.
- **Résumé PDF.** None is in the repo, so the page has no download.
